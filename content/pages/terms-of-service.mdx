## WhatAisle Store Subscriptions

The standard subscription is US$199 per month or US$1,999 per year. The INCAD promotion selects CA$199 per month or CA$1,999 per year. These prices exclude applicable taxes; the checkout shows the currency, taxes and total before payment. A subscription keeps its selected currency when changing between monthly and annual billing.

An eligible new store receives two bonus months once. The first monthly payment covers three calendar months; the first annual payment covers fourteen calendar months. The service period begins on the successful payment date. Later monthly payments cover one month and later annual payments cover twelve months. Switching plans, canceling or restoring service does not repeat the bonus.

The restricted 1CADTEST offer costs CA$1 per month, has no bonus months, and is limited to three successful subscription openings by designated test accounts. Its renewals do not use additional places. It continues at the test price until canceled and does not automatically become a standard-price subscription.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Owners can schedule a monthly or annual plan change in their dashboard. The change starts after the current paid and bonus period ends, with no immediate charge. Canceling renewal also cancels a scheduled plan change and preserves access through the already purchased and gifted period. Refund requests are reviewed manually; canceling or switching plans does not automatically issue a refund. Contact [support@whataisle.com](mailto:support@whataisle.com) for assistance.

If a renewal payment fails, we notify the account email and allow seven days of continued service. When service stops, public product finding and staff scanning are suspended. Data remains available for restoration for three calendar months from the actual service end, rather than from the cancellation request. Restoration during that period requires a new payment and begins a new service period on that payment date, without charging for the suspended time or granting another bonus.

After the retention period, store data becomes eligible for manual cleanup. We do not send a retention-expiry email. Cleanup ends self-service restoration; it is not an automatic deletion of all account, billing, backup or archived files. Contact us for questions about those records.
